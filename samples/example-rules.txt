Pinecone Valley Civic App Challenge — Official Rules

1. Dates
Submission Period: March 2, 2027 (9:00 am Pacific Time) – April 13, 2027 (11:59 pm Pacific Time).
Judging Period: April 15, 2027 (9:00 am Pacific Time) – April 30, 2027 (5:00 pm Pacific Time).
Winners will be announced on or around May 7, 2027 (12:00 pm Pacific Time).

2. Eligibility
The Challenge is open to individuals who are at least 18 years of age at the time of entry and to teams of up to four eligible individuals.
The Challenge is not open to employees of the Town of Pinecone Valley or members of their immediate family.

3. What to Submit
Each submission must include a working web or mobile app that helps residents use a town service.
You must provide a link to a public code repository that includes an open source license file.
You must include a demo video that is less than two (2) minutes long and publicly visible on YouTube.
You must include a written description of no more than 300 words.
Projects must be newly created during the Submission Period; previously published apps are not eligible.
A project that has been submitted to any other competition may not be entered in this Challenge.

4. Judging
Entries will be judged on three equally weighted criteria: usefulness to residents, ease of use, and quality of the demo.
Judges are not required to test the app and may judge based solely on the video and description.

5. Prizes
First place receives $3,000 and second place receives $1,000.
Each project is eligible for one (1) prize only.
