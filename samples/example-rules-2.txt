Harbor City Open Data Sprint — Official Rules

Submission Deadline: May 20, 2027 (5:00 pm Eastern Time).
The Sprint is open to individuals who are at least 18 years of age.
Projects may be newly created or, if the project existed prior to the Submission Period, must have been significantly updated during it.
You must provide a URL to a working demo or hosted application.
You must include a demo video under three (3) minutes, uploaded to YouTube or Vimeo.
You must provide a link to a public code repository with an open source license file.
Entries will be judged on impact, technical quality, and design, equally weighted.
Each project is eligible for one (1) prize.
