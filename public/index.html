What Fineprint does

  1. Reads a contest's official rules (URL or pasted text).
  2. Pulls out deadlines (in your time zone), eligibility, what to submit, how it's judged, and gotchas.
  3. Checks that every item's quote really appears in the rules, word for word. ✓ quote verified ⚠ quote not found
  4. Load a second contest and ask “Can I stack these?”
Fineprint quotes the rules; it doesn't give legal advice. Always read the source.
